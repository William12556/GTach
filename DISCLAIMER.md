## 2.0 Scope

GTach connects to a vehicle's OBD-II port and interoperates with the vehicle's
engine control systems. It is experimental software, not certified for use as
a primary or sole instrument. The RPM display, colour bands, and any other
readings may be inaccurate, delayed, or unavailable. Do not rely on GTach in
place of the vehicle's own instrumentation, and do not let its use distract
from safe operation of the vehicle.
