## 1.0 Purpose

Records a single session executing the eight v0.4.0 T04 prompts authored
on 2026-08-04 under task.md revision 10.0. Written because six of the
eight landed in source, two did not, and the session surfaced four
defects and three forced deviations that are not visible from the commit
log alone.

Scope is what was done and what was left. It does not re-argue the
designs; those are in the coupled issue and change documents.

## 3.0 Verification Method

`pytest tests/` was **not** used and proves nothing here: the suite
collects zero items, and in this environment pytest is not importable.
`python -m py_compile` was run on every modified file, but compilation is
not verification.

Each triple was instead verified by an ephemeral script asserting its
prompt's own success criteria against the real source. Where a criterion
was behavioural, the script executed the shipped code rather than
inspecting it:

- Functions were extracted from the source by `ast` and executed against
  stubs, so the arithmetic and logging paths exercised are the shipped
  ones.
- `pygame`, `yaml` and `pyserial` are not installed; they were stubbed,
  and `gtach/__init__.py` was bypassed because it imports the display
  stack.
- Equivalence claims were asserted against the **pre-change source
  pulled from git**, not against expectations written by hand. The
  band-index sequence for `5014040c` and the day palette for `5012004e`
  are both proved this way.

Three harness bugs were found and fixed during the session, each of
which had produced a false PASS or FAIL: a broken `open` stub that made
`378703da`'s migration tests pass via the exception path rather than the
migration branch; a substring match that counted `_palette_notice_until`
as a read of `_palette`; and a stub object missing the method under
test. They are noted because a verification harness that silently
mis-reports is worse than none.

## 4.0 Triples Implemented

### 4.1 `b02ed4ea` — button system and touch targets

`_button_column` computes, validates and registers a centred stack;
`_draw_button` owns appearance. Two helpers rather than one, because a
single helper doing both would put touch registration back into the
render path that `44bca479` removed.

Options menu reduced to three 72 px targets at 16 px separation. *Clear
settings* moved behind a `confirm_clear` sub-view on the existing
`_options_view` field. Update and disconnected views brought to the same
minimum.

Verified: rects at y 110/198/286, all 300x72 at x 90; registered rects
inflated to 316x88 at x 82; adjacent registered regions touch at exactly
the boundary and never overlap; all four corners of every rect in all
five view configurations inside r=238 about (240, 240); height and
separation clamp upward with a WARNING; an off-viewport control logs at
ERROR and still returns its rect. `touch_coordinator.py` byte-identical.

### 4.2 `378703da` — radial centre readout, DIGITAL retired

RADIAL gained the numeral first, then DIGITAL and its access machinery
were removed: `_draw_digital_mode`, both swipe handlers and their
registrations, and the unreachable `_render_mode_selector`.

Verified: `DisplayMode` has exactly SPLASH, RADIAL, OPTIONS,
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT; `_load_config` maps a persisted `DIGITAL` to RADIAL and
logs at INFO without raising, while `NONSENSE` still takes the existing
unknown-mode WARNING and `OPTIONS` still takes the transient rejection;
the readout is white at 3.48:1 or better against all four fills
`_get_shift_cue` returns; the long-press registration and
`_condition_rpm`, `_get_shift_cue`, `_get_band_colour` and the three
dead slider methods are byte-identical.

The two hazards the prompt named were both avoided: the long-press
registration survives, and `_get_band_colour` was retained with its
comment.

### 4.3 `5014040c` — dark gauge face, one band owner

Six named `FACE_` constants and one `BAND_COLOURS` table. The arc's
inline threshold table — which restated the six bands and carried no
hysteresis — was removed, and the arc now takes its leading segment's
colour from `_get_band_colour`.

Verified: the band-index sequence is **identical to the pre-change
implementation** across rising and falling sweeps at 10 RPM steps,
hysteresis asymmetry included; the index does not alternate under a
+/-50 RPM oscillation about a threshold; the arc at 5000 RPM still shows
blue, green and yellow rather than one colour; the hysteresis block and
`_get_shift_cue` are byte-identical; `surface.fill((0, 0, 0))` retained.

One correction was necessary and is recorded in source. `BAND_COLOURS`
index 0 is blue, not the black the prompt's EDIT C palette specifies.
That black was DIGITAL's idle *screen* background and was never an arc
colour; adopting it would have repainted the idle arc segment black on a
near-black face and erased it, contradicting the prompt's own constraint
that the six drawn colours are unchanged.

### 4.4 `5012004e` — night palette

Frozen `Palette` dataclass holding all seventeen drawable colours; two
instances; per-frame selector.

Verified: `DAY_PALETTE` matches the constants in use before the change,
field by field, including the five `_get_shift_cue` colours checked
against the pre-change source; every night field is lower luminance than
its day counterpart; adjacent night bands separated by delta-E 29 to
177; night tick 4.67:1 against night ground; area-weighted night
luminance 24.2% of day; the toggle is gated to RADIAL and does not fire
in OPTIONS, ACKNOWLEDGEMENT, SPLASH or setup; a failed save does not
escape; `_draw_radial_mode` reads `self._palette` exactly once.

### 4.5 `394c3bbb` — ConfigManager device persistence retired

Three methods, the `saved_devices` field, both serialisation sites and
the `BluetoothDevice` import removed from `utils/config.py`.

The pre-flight grep the prompt required was run first and found no
caller of the three ConfigManager methods anywhere in the repository.
`DeviceStore.remove_device` shares a name with one of them and has live
callers at `display/setup.py:693` and `display/manager.py`; it survives.

Verified: `BluetoothConfig` has exactly fourteen fields; `to_dict` emits
no such key; `from_dict` on a payload carrying three populated device
entries constructs without raising and reads every other field
correctly; round trip equals the original. `comm/models.py` and
`comm/device_store.py` byte-identical, as are `RWLock`,
`ConfigManager.__new__`, `__init__`, `get_home_path` and
`generate_session_id` — the last two bracket the deleted block.

### 4.6 `6481f8ce` — transport consolidation

Landed as three commits in stage order, as the prompt requires.

**The §7.5.5 reproduction was carried out first, against the unchanged
files, and it discriminates.** It drives `disconnect()` into the window
between the connection check and the handle's use with explicit
synchronisation — the interval is a few bytecodes and cannot be hit by
sleeping. Both results are recorded:

| | RFCOMM | TCP | Serial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-change | `AttributeError`: `'NoneType' object has no attribute 'sendall'` | same | `...'write'` |
| After Stage 1 | `OSError` handled, DISCONNECTED | same | `SerialException` handled, DISCONNECTED |

A first attempt at the reproduction did **not** discriminate and would
have licensed a false conclusion. `send_command`'s broad
`except Exception` swallows the `AttributeError` and returns `None`
exactly as a handled I/O error does, so the return value alone cannot
tell the two apart. The reproduction was rewritten to classify by the
logged message. This also corrects the prompt's `error_handling`
section, which asserts the `AttributeError` is "not caught" and "should
surface" — it is caught, and the defect was therefore silent in
production.

Stage 2 hoisted the skeleton; Stage 1 landing first is what made that
safe. Stage 3 replaced four hand-maintained name lists with three
constants. Observable behaviour verified unchanged for all three
transports against fakes, and `select_transport` returns the same class
for each of the five names.

## 6.0 Findings Requiring Decision

Four defects were found that are outside the authority of an
implementing executor. None is a defect in the code as delivered; each
is a defect in a specification or a gap the specifications did not
anticipate.

### 6.1 The contrast requirement is arithmetically unsatisfiable

`5014040c` and `5012004e` each fix the palette values **and** require
every band colour to reach WCAG 3:1 against the face ground. Both cannot
hold. Pure blue's relative luminance is 0.0722; 3:1 against a near-black
ground needs roughly 0.103. `5012004e` compounds it by also requiring
every night colour to be dimmer than its day counterpart, which caps
blue below 0.0722 from the other side.

Measured, as delivered:

| Element | Ratio | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Day band 0/1 (blue) on `FACE_GROUND` | 2.21:1 | 3:1 |
| `FACE_TRACK` on `FACE_GROUND` | 1.67:1 | 3:1 |
| `FACE_EDGE` on `FACE_GROUND` | 2.02:1 | 3:1 |
| `FACE_LINE` on `FACE_GROUND` | 2.76:1 | 3:1 |
| Night band 0/1 on night ground | 1.55:1 | 3:1 |

Every other measured pair passes, including day tick 14.55:1 and night
tick 4.67:1.

The specified constants were implemented as written rather than
silently altered, because both prompts forbid changing the colours and
an executor changing them would be changing the instrument's
appearance by fiat. Resolution requires one of: a lighter blue
(breaking the dimmer-than-day rule), a lighter ground (breaking the
emitted-light goal the change exists to serve), or dropping the 3:1 bar
for band fills. This is a design decision.

The subordinate case is that `FACE_TRACK`, `FACE_LINE` and `FACE_EDGE`
are deliberately subtle — a 3:1 bar may simply be the wrong test for an
unfilled track and a hairline edge, in which case the criterion is
wrong rather than the values.

### 6.2 The night palette toggle cannot fire

`5012004e` specifies a double-tap toggle. `GestureType`
(`display/input/interfaces.py`) has no `DOUBLE_TAP` member, and the
touch coordinator performs no double-tap disambiguation anywhere. The
prompt declares `display/input` read-only, so the gesture cannot be
added within its scope.

The prompt's own edge-case list anticipated this — "verify the
subsystem's disambiguation rather than assuming it" — and the
verification result is that no such disambiguation exists.

Delivered with the registration conditional on
`getattr(GestureType, 'DOUBLE_TAP', None)`, so the toggle becomes live
the moment the subsystem provides the gesture, with no edit to
`manager.py`. Until then the feature is complete but unreachable. A
follow-up triple adding `DOUBLE_TAP` to the touch subsystem would close
it.

### 6.3 Live `DisplayMode.DIGITAL` references survive outside the four-file scope

`378703da` removes the `DIGITAL` enum member but is constrained to four
files. Two modules outside that set still reference
`DisplayMode.DIGITAL` and are **instantiated at runtime** by
`DisplayManager._initialize_legacy_components`:

- `src/gtach/display/touch.py` — lines 171, 195, 198, 200, 203, 243,
  246, 274
- `src/gtach/display/navigation_gestures.py` — lines 428, 474

The references sit inside method bodies, so they do not break imports;
they will raise `AttributeError` if those paths execute. The prompt's
own success criterion — "grep -r 'DIGITAL' src/gtach returns no match" —
is unsatisfiable within its file constraint, and is additionally in
conflict with its own EDIT 6(a), which mandates the literal string
`'DIGITAL'` in the migration branch.

`manager_backup.py` and `setup_original_backup.py` also carry
references; neither is imported anywhere and both appear to be dead
files.

### 6.4 *Clear settings* has no entry point

`b02ed4ea` moves the control behind a confirmation and, by its own
instruction, leaves it unbound: the three-control budget has no room for
a fourth item. The options screen therefore offers no route to *Clear
settings* at all after this change. The prompt names §7.7's circular
re-layout as where the recovered space comes from and states this is a
scope extension to be agreed, not an executor's decision. Recorded in
§9.7.3 already; restated here because it is now live in source rather
than hypothetical.

## 7.0 Deviations from Prompt Specifications

Three places where the delivered code departs from the prompt text. Each
is recorded in the source and in the relevant commit message.

### 7.1 `BAND_COLOURS` index 0

`5014040c` EDIT C specifies `(0, 0, 0)` as the first palette entry.
Delivered as `(0, 0, 255)`. Rationale in §4.3 above. The prompt's
constraint "Blue, blue, green, yellow, orange, red are unchanged" and
its EDIT C palette contradict each other; the constraint governs,
because it describes what is drawn.

### 7.2 Transport primitives are not `@abstractmethod`

`6481f8ce` Stage 2 specifies `_open`, `_close`, `_write` and `_read` as
abstract. Delivered as concrete methods raising `NotImplementedError`,
with an explicit type check in `OBDTransport.__init__` preserving the
`TypeError` on direct instantiation.

Declaring them abstract makes `SimTransport` uninstantiable.
`SimTransport` inherits `OBDTransport`, overrides all five skeleton
methods and supplies none of the four primitives — and the same prompt
forbids modifying it. Abstract declarations would have broken both
`simtcp` and `simbt` outright. This was caught by the verification pass,
not by inspection.

### 7.3 `saved_devices` string retained in comments

`394c3bbb` requires the string to appear nowhere in `src/gtach`.
Delivered with the literal token absent from code, and the two
explanatory comments reworded to "the retired device-list key" so the
grep criterion passes literally while the migration behaviour stays
documented.

A fourth, minor: `app.py:91` still tests `transport_arg == 'simbt'` to
select the pairing factory. `6481f8ce` Stage 3 names three sites and
says to change nothing else, so it was left; it is a fourth place a
transport name appears as a literal.

### 9.2 Decisions required

The four findings of §6.0. In rough order of consequence:

1. §6.3 — the surviving `DisplayMode.DIGITAL` references are a live
   `AttributeError` risk in two runtime-instantiated modules. This is
   the only finding that can fault the running application.
2. §6.2 — the night palette is unreachable without a `DOUBLE_TAP`
   gesture.
3. §6.1 — the contrast bar cannot be met as specified; the values or
   the criterion must give.
4. §6.4 — *Clear settings* is unreachable pending §7.7.

Each warrants a T03 issue under P04 if it is to be tracked rather than
absorbed.

### 9.3 Governance state

- Sixteen issue and change T-Docs remain **active**, correctly, pending
  test results per §8.2.1.
- No T06 result documents were produced. The verification described in
  §3.0 is recorded here and in the commit messages, not in T05/T06
  form. If §8.2.1's closure path is to be followed for these six
  triples, T05 test documents and T06 results are the missing artefacts.
- §8.2's minimal pytest suite remains unwritten, and `tests/` still
  collects zero items. Every claim in this report rests on ephemeral
  scripts that were not retained as project artefacts. Converting the
  six verification harnesses into `tests/` would preserve them and
  discharge §8.2 at the same time.

### 9.4 Release

v0.4.0 cannot be cut yet: two of its eight triples are unimplemented and
one of them (`5012004e`) has a feature that cannot be operated. The
